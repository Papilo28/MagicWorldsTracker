FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really charge zero transaction fees?
+
Yes, 100%. We charge a flat monthly subscription and take nothing from your affiliate sales. Every dollar your affiliates generate belongs to you. No percentages, no hidden fees, no surprises.
How does the $0.10 card verification work?
+
We charge $0.10 to verify your payment card when you start your free trial. This micro-charge is refunded immediately after verification. It lets us confirm your card is valid so we can activate your trial without friction. You will not be billed anything further until your 14-day trial ends.
How does Web3 tracking actually work?
+
We use a dual-layer system: a JavaScript snippet on your frontend detects affiliate codes from URL parameters and stores them in cookies; and a REST API endpoint your backend calls to record on-chain events like wallet connections, NFT mints, and token swaps. You pass the affiliate code and event type and we handle attribution, commission calculation, and reporting.
Can affiliates receive crypto payouts?
+
Yes. Each affiliate gets an auto-generated Ethereum-compatible wallet address when they sign up. They can also add their own wallet address. You can pay affiliates in crypto or fiat. We generate payout reports so you know exactly who gets what and how much.
How long does setup take?
+
Most founders go from signup to first live affiliate link in under 30 minutes. Create your merchant account, configure your commission rate, share your signup page with affiliates. No developer required for basic setup. Advanced S2S tracking and API integration typically takes a developer 1 to 2 hours.
Do you support recurring commissions for subscriptions?
+
Yes, on Pro and Scale plans. You can configure a subscription conversion event type and your affiliates will be credited each billing cycle a referred customer pays. Perfect for SaaS, NFT membership clubs, and any subscription-based product.
Can I white-label the affiliate dashboard?
+
White-labeling with your own domain and branding is available on the Scale plan. Your affiliates will see your brand throughout their entire experience, from signup page to dashboard to emails. Contact us if you need custom domain setup help.
